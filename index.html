DESERT TOMBSA LIGHT PUZZLE

Bring light into the dark.

Deep under the sand, the tomb chambers wait in darkness. Place mirrors, crystals, coloured glass and prisms, bend a single beam of sunlight around pillars and walls, and wake every eye in the room.

The puzzle

MIRRORS AND CRYSTALS

Turn the light around corners, or split it in two and send it down both paths at once.

COLOURED GLASS

Some eyes wake only to red, green or blue. Filter the beam through glass on its way, and mix colours where beams meet.

PRISMS

Break white light into its colours and send each one where it is needed.

SCARAB COINS

Every chamber hides three golden scarabs. Light them all on the way to the answer for a perfect solve.

Race with friends

2 TO 6 PLAYERS

Create a private room and share its four-letter code. Everyone gets the same eight boards, and the first to light each one takes the points. The last two boards are worth double.

THE SCARAB

On a few boards a single scarab sits on a glowing tile. The first light to reach it earns a bonus point, so a quick detour can turn the race.

PRACTICE WITH BOTS

Learn the race against computer players, right on your phone.
