Privacy Policy — Desert Tombs

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Desert Tombs is a game for iPhone. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code. This policy explains what it keeps and what it sends.

What the game stores on your device

Which chambers you have lit and how many scarabs you found in each, your sound and music switches, which lessons and tips you have seen, the username you set in Settings, the boards you last picked for a race, the random player codes that keep your seat in a room, a copy of the identifier Apple gives this developer’s apps on your device (used only to notice player codes restored from another phone’s backup; it never leaves your phone), and, while a race is going, the room you are in so you can rejoin it. They are kept in the app’s own storage on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Racing with friends

When you create or join a room, the game connects to our room server, hosted by Cloudflare. It sends the username you choose for that room, the room’s code, random codes the game makes on your device (so you keep your seat if the connection drops, and no one else can take it), and your moves on each board. The room uses them to run the race and show the scores to the players in that room, and to no one else. Cloudflare runs the room server for us as our hosting provider and handles this data only to deliver the service.

A room’s data is deleted when the room is closed, or, if nobody closes it, after two hours without play (ten minutes if every player has left it on purpose before or after a race). We do not store your IP address (Cloudflare’s network may process it briefly to deliver and protect the service). Pick a username that doesn’t identify you if you prefer; it does not need to be your name. The room server turns away usernames that contain rude words.

Practice with bots runs entirely on your phone and sends nothing.

Your choices

When the host closes a room, its data is deleted at once. If you leave a room before the race starts, your seat is removed; if you leave during or after a race, your username and score stay until the room is closed or expires. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone. To ask about your data or have anything deleted sooner, email apps.saleh@pm.me.

Permissions

Desert Tombs does not ask for your location, camera, photos, contacts, microphone, tracking or notifications.

Apple-provided data

The App Store gives developers aggregated, anonymous statistics such as download counts and crash reports, depending on your device’s analytics-sharing setting. These never identify you.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
